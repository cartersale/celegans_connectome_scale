# Pre-registration — fly hemilineage sub-split fragility curve

Written and fixed **before any preservation number was computed**. The worm
analysis rested on having no discretion over the partition; the moment the
sub-splitting rule is tuned against preservation, that property is lost. Nothing
below may be changed after seeing results. Deviations, if forced, get recorded in
a dated amendment section at the bottom with the reason.

## Why this and not a generalisation panel

The worm establishes that the biological partition is lossless and size-matched
randoms are destructive, but only across 1x-2.5x compression, because 302 neurons
over 119 classes is all the dynamic range there is. (Checked: the C. elegans class
partition is the finest biological grouping available — below it are individual
neurons — so no sub-split ladder exists in the worm, and the existing merge
trajectory already covers the full range. The worm result is therefore already a
curve, not a point; its limitation is range, not resolution of the x-axis.)

The fly hemilineages give 175x compression at clean provenance, so sub-splitting
them produces a family of lineage-derived partitions spanning roughly 175x down
toward 5x. That answers the question the worm cannot: is biology lossless at
*every* scale, or only at the particular scale the classical typing happened to
choose.

## Scope decision

Central brain only: `super_class == "central"`, restricted to neurons with
`ito_lee_hemilineage` assigned (32,327 of 32,383 = 99.8%). This is a **scope
decision, not a computational one** — the central brain is where connectivity-
independent typing exists at all. Optic (0.2% hemilineage coverage), sensory (0%)
and ascending (0%) are out of scope because they have no clean partition to test.

## Provenance tiers — two rungs of cleanliness, labelled as such

- **Tier A (unimpeachable, lineage-derived).** `ito_lee_hemilineage`; and
  hemilineage x `side`. Assigned from neuroblast lineage and developmental
  origin, prior to any connectivity.
- **Tier B (morphology-derived).** Sub-splits of Tier A cells by soma position
  (below). NBLAST-style morphological similarity is not connectivity, so the
  provenance argument holds, but morphology and connectivity covary through
  neuropil overlap. Tier B points are plotted and labelled as morphology-derived;
  Tier A points are the anchors.

Explicitly excluded from every rung: `cell_type`, `hemibrain_type`, `supertype`,
`cell_class`. These used connectivity in their assignment and would reopen the
circularity objection closed in the worm.

## The sub-splitting rule (fixed, applied blind)

1. **Side first.** Partition each hemilineage by `side`. Deterministic, no
   parameters.
2. **Then morphological clustering within each (hemilineage, side) cell:**
   - features: `soma_x`, `soma_y`, `soma_z`, z-scored across the in-scope neuron
     set (one scaling for the whole set, not per group)
   - method: `scipy.cluster.hierarchy.linkage(method="ward", metric="euclidean")`
   - cut: `fcluster(criterion="maxclust", t=max(1, round(f * n)))` for a group of
     size `n`
   - the ladder is generated by a **single global parameter** `f` in (0, 1],
     swept over a fixed grid: f = 0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.30, 0.40,
     0.55, 0.70, 0.85, 1.00. f = 1 is all-singletons; the Tier A rungs sit at
     the coarse end.
3. Ward linkage on z-scored soma coordinates is chosen because soma position is
   carried in the released annotation table, is purely anatomical, and needs no
   connectivity. Recorded limitation: soma position is a coarser morphological
   proxy than arbor-shape (NBLAST) similarity. This is a stated weakness, not
   something to be revisited if the curve disappoints.

No other clustering method, linkage, feature set, or cut criterion will be tried.

## Randoms

At **every** rung, the null is a partition with the **identical cell-size
profile**, produced by permuting neuron labels within the in-scope set. Same
discipline as the worm; this is what makes the curve interpretable rather than a
comparison across unmatched granularities.

## Propagation operator and source sampling

Operator: resolvent `(I - alpha*A/rho)^-1 - I` on the weighted directed
connectivity. At 32k neurons the dense inverse is out, so one **sparse solve per
source neuron** gives that source's full propagation column.

Sources are sampled **stratified by out-degree decile**, equal numbers per decile,
because propagation columns vary a lot in density and a uniform sample would be
dominated by low-out-degree neurons. The number of sources and the realised
per-decile counts are reported with the result.

## Preservation metric

No brain-wide causal propagation measurement exists in the fly, so preservation
is scored **against the full-resolution operator itself**, not against measured
responses. This is a structural claim and is weaker than the worm's, which is
grounded in measured responses. It will be described that way in the text.

`loss(k) = 1 - rho_s( quotient operator values , full operator values )` over the
sampled ordered pairs, where the quotient value for a pair is read at the pair's
cells. Biological and random arms scored on identical pair samples.

## Pre-committed directional prediction (depth)

**The informative alpha is larger in the fly than in the worm.** In the worm the
benefit plateaued by alpha ~ 0.001-0.1 and two hops carried nearly the whole
effect. Two synapses do not span the fly brain, so deeper aggregation should
carry information there. Committed before running. If the fly optimum is at or
below the worm's, that is recorded as a failed prediction, not reinterpreted.

## Amendments

### 2026-10-08 — propagation operator computed by truncated series

The operator section above specifies one sparse solve per source neuron. The
implementation (`src/conres/flywire.py`, `propagation_rows`) instead sums the
series `sum_t (alpha*A/rho)^t` with sparse matrix products up to the first path
length t with alpha^t <= 1e-6 (t = 6 at alpha = 0.1). On the worm connectome
this reproduces the exact inverse to within 1.8e-8 (Spearman 0.99998), and
the truncation error is bounded by the discarded terms of the same geometric
series in the fly. The sub-splitting rule, f grid, source sampling and loss metric are
as written above. This amendment was recorded when the discrepancy was found
during a code audit; it does not change any analysis choice.
